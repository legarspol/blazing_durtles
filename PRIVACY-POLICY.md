# Privacy Policy for Blazing Durtles

Effective date: 2026-07-23

## Introduction

Blazing Durtles ("us", "we", or "our") operates as a third-party client application for the WaniKani service. The developer and publisher of the application is the Blazing Durtles open source contributor ("developer"). This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Blazing Durtles Android application (the "App").

We take your privacy seriously and are committed to ensuring that your personal information is protected. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information We Collect

### WaniKani API Key

The App collects your WaniKani API key. This API key is necessary for accessing WaniKani's service and enabling the functionality of the App. The API key is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to or shared with any third parties other than WaniKani itself.

### Optional Analytics and Crash Reporting

The App includes optional analytics and crash reporting features, provided by Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. These features are disabled by default and are only active if you choose to enable them in the App settings. You can change your choice at any time.

If you enable these features, the following information may be collected and processed by Google:

- **Google Analytics for Firebase:** anonymized usage data such as screens viewed, features used, session duration, app version, device model, operating system version, and country-level location. This helps us understand how the App is used and improve it.
- **Firebase Crashlytics:** crash reports and diagnostic information, including stack traces, device state at the time of a crash, device model, and operating system version. This helps us identify and fix bugs.

These services also generate identifiers in order to function: Google Analytics for Firebase uses an app instance ID, and Firebase Crashlytics uses a Crashlytics installation UUID. These identifiers are tied to your installation of the App, not to your name or identity, and are reset if you reinstall the App.

Your WaniKani API key is never included in analytics or crash reporting data.

For more information on how Google processes this data, please see:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

If you leave these features disabled, the App does not collect any analytics, usage data, or other identifiers.

## How Your Information is Used

Your WaniKani API key is used solely to authenticate your access to WaniKani's services and to fetch data necessary for the App's functionality.

If you have enabled the optional analytics and crash reporting features, the collected data is used solely to improve the stability and usability of the App. It is never used for advertising and is never sold.

## Data Sharing

The App does not share your API key with any third parties. As a client for the WaniKani service, the App only interacts with WaniKani's API to provide its features.

If you have enabled the optional analytics and crash reporting features, the data described above is shared with Google, which acts as a data processor for these services. No other data is shared with any third parties.

## Data Retention

Your WaniKani API key is stored locally on your device and remains there until you sign out or uninstall the App.

If you have enabled the optional analytics and crash reporting features, event-level analytics data is retained by Google for 2 months and user-level analytics data for 14 months. Crash reporting data is retained for 90 days. After these periods the data is automatically deleted. Disabling these features in the App settings stops any further collection.

## International Data Transfers

If you have enabled the optional analytics and crash reporting features, the data described above is processed by Google and may be transferred to and stored on servers located outside your country of residence, including outside the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area. Google safeguards these transfers through mechanisms such as its standard contractual clauses. For details, see Google's data processing terms at https://firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms.

## Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under applicable data protection law (such as the UK GDPR and EU GDPR), including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time.

You can exercise these rights as follows:

- **Withdraw consent:** disable analytics and crash reporting at any time in the App settings. This stops all further collection.
- **Delete local data:** sign out of the App or uninstall it to remove your WaniKani API key from your device.
- **Analytics and crash data:** because this data is tied to installation identifiers rather than your identity, we are not able to look up or identify data belonging to a specific person. Data already collected is automatically deleted at the end of the retention periods described in the Data Retention section.

If you have any questions or wish to make a request regarding your data, please contact us using the method described in the Contact Us section. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority, such as the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the United Kingdom.

## Third-Party Links

The App may include links to external websites or services, such as the WaniKani website. These external sites are not operated by us. We strongly encourage you to review the privacy policy of every website or service you visit. We are not responsible for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

## Security

We take appropriate measures to protect the security of your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
